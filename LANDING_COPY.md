## FAQ

**1. Is this really free?**  
Yes. The full audit and shareable results are free. We may ask for email if you want a copy sent to you or to hear about discounted AI credits—that’s optional unless you choose it.

**2. What do you do with my data?**  
We use what you enter to generate the audit and (if you opt in) to follow up. Don’t paste API keys or confidential customer data—amounts and plan names are enough. See our privacy policy for retention and deletion.

**3. How accurate is the pricing data?**  
We track **public list pricing** and common plan names; your **actual** bill may reflect annual discounts, credits, or enterprise quotes. Treat outputs as **directional**, not a legal invoice—then verify with your vendor.

**4. Do I need to sign up?**  
No account required to run an audit. Signing up is only if we offer save-history or email delivery and you want it.

**5. What is Credex?**  
Credex sells **discounted AI credits** to companies that already spend heavily on models and APIs. SpendSight is an independent audit; if you’re a fit, we may mention Credex after your results—not before you see savings math.
